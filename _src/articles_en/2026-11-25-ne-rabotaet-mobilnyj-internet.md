<p class="lead"><b>In short:</b> if your mobile data in Russia suddenly stops, or only Gosuslugi, banks and VK still open, your phone is usually fine. Most often it's a regional restriction: a full shutdown or whitelist mode. Less often it's a carrier outage or a phone setting. The <a href="/en/check/">connection check</a> shows the cause in 5 seconds. Below is what to do in each case.</p>

## Why mobile internet isn't working

| What you see | Most likely |
|---|---|
| Nothing loads, but the 4G icon is there | internet is shut down in your area or the carrier has an outage |
| Only Gosuslugi, banks, VK, Yandex and marketplaces open | whitelist mode is on |
| Speed dropped to 2G or "E" | speed is throttled in your area |
| Only one site or app fails | that service is blocked |
| Wi-Fi works, friends on the same carrier have no data either | the problem is the network, not your phone |
| Friends' phones work, yours doesn't | phone settings, balance or used-up data plan |

Since 2025 Russian regions regularly restrict mobile internet. Officials say it's to protect against drones. During these restrictions only sites and apps on the government whitelist keep working. Our <a href="/en/status/">outage status page</a> has a fresh summary by region and carrier every morning.

## What to do: a 2-minute check

1. **See what exactly fails.** Open the <a href="/en/check/">connection check</a> on mobile data (turn Wi-Fi off) and tap the whitelist tab. In 5 seconds you'll know: full shutdown, whitelist mode or just some services blocked.
2. **Toggle airplane mode on and off.** The phone reconnects to the tower. Sometimes that's enough for an ordinary network glitch.
3. **Check your balance and data allowance** in the carrier's app. A used-up plan looks exactly like a shutdown.
4. **Make sure mobile data is on** and the right SIM is selected if you have two.
5. **Restart the phone.** It clears stuck network settings.
6. **Check for a mass outage.** We keep daily summaries for <a href="/en/operator/mts/">MTS</a>, <a href="/en/operator/beeline/">Beeline</a>, <a href="/en/operator/megafon/">MegaFon</a> and <a href="/en/operator/t2/">T2</a>.

## If whitelist mode is on

In whitelist mode the carrier only lets approved services through. Telegram, YouTube, WhatsApp, most foreign sites and ordinary VPNs won't open. What you can do:

- **Use Wi-Fi.** Home internet has no whitelists, the restrictions only cover mobile networks.
- **Wait for the all-clear.** Restrictions are usually lifted after a few hours, but sometimes they last longer.
- **Don't expect a regular VPN to help:** it simply won't connect in this mode. How whitelists work and how to spot them: <a href="/en/blog/belye-spiski-chto-eto/">mobile internet whitelists in Russia</a>.

## If it only fails with a VPN

If mobile data works without the VPN but nothing loads with it, the VPN is the problem: an outdated subscription, a blocked server or protocol. Step-by-step fixes: <a href="/en/blog/ne-rabotaet-vpn-na-mobilnom/">VPN not working on mobile data</a>.

## If data stopped on your iPhone or Android

When friends on the same carrier are fine, check your phone:

- **iPhone:** Settings, Mobile Data (Cellular): make sure it's on and the app you need is allowed below. If that doesn't help: General, Transfer or Reset iPhone, Reset, Reset Network Settings.
- **Android:** Settings, Connections (or Network and internet), Mobile network, Mobile data. Make sure data limits and data saver are off.

## FAQ

### Why is mobile internet not working in Russia today?

Most often because of a regional restriction: a full shutdown or whitelist mode. Check <a href="/en/status/">today's status</a> or run the <a href="/en/check/">check</a> to see what's happening on your connection.

### Why does Wi-Fi work but mobile data doesn't?

The restrictions only cover mobile networks. Home internet and Wi-Fi keep working as usual. If that's your case, it's almost certainly a shutdown or whitelist mode at your carrier, not a broken phone.

### When will mobile internet come back?

Usually no timing is announced. Restrictions are lifted when, according to officials, the situation allows, most often after a few hours.

### Which sites work during a mobile internet shutdown?

Whitelisted services: Gosuslugi, VK, Yandex, Mail.ru, major banks, marketplaces and Max. Telegram, YouTube and WhatsApp are not on the whitelist.
