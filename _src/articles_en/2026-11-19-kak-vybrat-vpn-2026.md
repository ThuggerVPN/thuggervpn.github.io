<p class="lead"><b>In short:</b> in 2026 what matters isn't the number of countries or speed-test results, but how fast the service comes back after the next blocking wave.</p>

## 7 criteria

| # | Criterion | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Modern protocol with traffic disguise | Look for VLESS/Reality, Hysteria2 or similar. Only WireGuard or OpenVPN is a bad sign |
| 2 | Fast reaction to blocks | Ask support how long it takes them to replace servers |
| 3 | Free trial | At least 1-3 days free to test on your carrier |
| 4 | Live support | Ask a question before paying - the answer should come within an hour or two |
| 5 | Works on mobile data | Test during the trial on your carrier, not just on Wi-Fi |
| 6 | Easy payment from Russia | Russian cards or the SBP fast-payment system without complicated schemes |
| 7 | Multiple devices | Phone and computer on one subscription |

## What to avoid

- **Free VPNs with no clear owner.** A service has to earn money somehow - if not from subscriptions, then from your data.
- **Apps with a history of "removed from the store".** That's normal in 2026, but the service should offer a way to connect without the app store, e.g. via a Telegram bot and a standard client.
- **Promises that it "always works everywhere".** An honest service admits outages happen and shows how fast it fixes them.

## Where to read reviews

Use independent catalogs where users vote by carrier - for example, [vpnstatus.site](https://vpnstatus.site): it shows whether a service works on MTS, MegaFon, Beeline and T2, and separately whether it bypasses whitelists.

## Tip: keep two VPNs

A main one and a backup - on different protocols from different providers. When one drops, the other will most likely keep working.

## FAQ

### Which VPN protocol works best in Russia in 2026?
Protocols that disguise traffic as regular HTTPS hold up best, e.g. VLESS with Reality. WireGuard and OpenVPN are blocked almost everywhere.

### Is a paid VPN worth it if free ones exist?
Free services get blocked more often, have no support, and it's unclear how they make money. A paid VPN at 200-300 ₽ a month usually runs more stably and recovers faster after blocks.

### How do I test a VPN before buying?
Take the free trial and test it on your carrier's mobile data for 2-3 days, not just on home Wi-Fi.
