## St Petersburg: outages at night and in the morning

St Petersburg and the surrounding Leningrad region had the most events.

- **Overnight into 20 November** mobile internet stopped working properly, with complaints peaking around 1 am. Some residents could only open whitelisted sites (according to the St Petersburg TV channel).
- **On the morning of 20 November** complaints rose again, to about 4,900 in a day, and some people lost Wi-Fi too.
- **On 21 November** only whitelisted services worked again in the morning. The same day mobile internet was shut down because of a drone threat: the Leningrad region governor reported 73 drones downed overnight.

The pattern is simple: restrictions go on after a drone warning and come off after the all-clear. That's why things can work fine during the day and fail at night or early in the morning.

## Shutdowns in other regions

On 21 November shutdowns and speed limits linked to drone threats were reported in Udmurtia, the Irkutsk, Pskov, Tyumen and Novosibirsk regions, Krasnoyarsk and Khakassia. On 22 November complaints came from dozens of regions, from Moscow to Krasnodar, across different carriers. Same cause: drone alerts.

## Carriers

All four got complaints:

- **MegaFon:** Arkhangelsk and Lipetsk (19 and 22 November).
- **Beeline:** Smolensk and Vologda regions, then Kirov, Vladimir and Samara, almost 500 complaints in a day on 21 November.
- **MTS:** about 300 complaints in a day on 21 November, mostly from the Perm region and St Petersburg.
- **T2:** Tatarstan and Ulyanovsk region, and on 22 November the Perm, Chelyabinsk and Sverdlovsk regions.

No carrier officially confirmed an outage. When restrictions are imposed at the authorities' request, every carrier is affected the same way, so switching carriers usually doesn't help.

## Services

On 20 November Telegram had problems from 7:15 am Moscow time: messages wouldn't send and media wouldn't load. On the evening of 21 November YouTube had disruptions across the country. If either is giving you trouble, see our guides: [Telegram not connecting](/en/blog/telegram-ne-podklyuchaetsya/) and [YouTube not working even with a VPN](/en/blog/youtube-ne-rabotaet-s-vpn/).

## What this means for you

1. Keep Wi-Fi within reach: home and office connections are usually restricted less than mobile data.
2. If your mobile data suddenly opens only banks and Gosuslugi, that's whitelist mode, not a broken phone. You can confirm it with our [connection check](/en/check/).
3. Download what you need (maps, documents, videos) in advance, especially if drone alerts are frequent where you are.

## FAQ

### Why does the internet go down at night?
According to media reports, restrictions are switched on during drone threats. In St Petersburg this week complaints peaked around 1 and 3 am and in the morning. Service usually comes back after the all-clear.

### Will switching carriers help?
If the restriction is ordered by the authorities, it applies to every carrier in the region. This week there were complaints about MTS, Beeline, MegaFon and T2.
