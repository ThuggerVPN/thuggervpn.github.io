<p class="lead"><b>In short:</b> a whitelist is a mode where mobile internet only opens pre-approved sites and apps. Everything else, including regular VPNs, stops working until the mode is lifted.</p>

## How it works

Regular blocking uses a blacklist: whatever is on the list is banned. A whitelist is the opposite: only what's on the list is allowed. During restrictions, mobile internet switches to this mode.

- **Mobile internet only.** Russia's Ministry of Digital Development has said the mechanism applies to mobile networks, not home internet.
- **The list keeps growing.** According to the Ministry, as of 12 November 2026 about 900 resources were on the whitelist: government services, banks, marketplaces, major Russian services.
- **It switches on without warning.** Carriers sometimes send notices in advance, but usually the mode just appears.

## How to tell you're on a whitelist

1. Gosuslugi, Yandex, Wildberries or Ozon open.
2. Telegram, YouTube and foreign sites don't.
3. The VPN won't connect, or connects with no traffic.
4. Everything works fine on Wi-Fi.

If three or four match, it's a whitelist.

## What you can do

- **Switch to Wi-Fi.** The most reliable option: home internet isn't restricted by this mode.
- **Wait.** The mode is usually temporary — from a few hours to a day.
- **Use a VPN with whitelist bypass.** It's a rare and expensive technology: it needs special servers, and such setups get shut down regularly. If a service promises a bypass, test it during the free trial before paying.

## FAQ

### Are whitelists applied to home internet too?
According to the Ministry and carriers — no, the mechanism concerns mobile internet. So during restrictions, connect to Wi-Fi first.

### Why does Yandex work but my VPN doesn't?
Specific services are added to the whitelist. VPN servers aren't on it, so a regular VPN can't establish a connection while the mode is on.

### How long does whitelist mode last?
It varies: from a few hours to a few days, depending on the region and the reason for the restrictions.
