<p class="lead"><b>In short:</b> without a VPN, Telegram in Russia mostly doesn't work. Russia started throttling it in February 2026, and by April it was blocked almost completely. Messages may get through now and then, but media, calls and channels are unreliable. If your plans, tickets or family chats live in Telegram, set up a VPN at home, save the key information offline and keep a second way to reach people.</p>

## What happened to Telegram in Russia

- **August 2025:** Roskomnadzor, the Russian telecom regulator, started restricting voice and video calls in Telegram and WhatsApp.
- **10 February 2026:** Roskomnadzor confirmed it was slowing Telegram down across the country. Photos and videos began to load slowly, then everything did.
- **10 April 2026:** monitoring services and the OONI research project recorded close to 100% failed connections to Telegram from Russia. In practice, the app stopped working without a workaround.
- **Late November 2026:** reports are mixed. On 27 November some users said Telegram suddenly worked without a VPN, while others complained the app hung on "Updating" and that proxies had stopped working. Treat any "it's back" news as temporary.

Telegram is also not on the mobile **whitelist**: the list of approved Russian services that stay available when mobile internet is restricted for security reasons. During such shutdowns, which happen often in many regions, Telegram won't work on mobile data even if it worked an hour earlier.

## What works and what doesn't

| Situation | Without a VPN | With a VPN |
|---|---|---|
| Russian SIM, normal mobile data | usually fails | usually works |
| Foreign SIM or eSIM in roaming | unreliable, depends on the provider | the most reliable combination |
| Hotel or café Wi-Fi | usually fails | usually works |
| Mobile data during a local shutdown | nothing works | usually nothing works, use Wi-Fi |
| Telegram calls | treat as unavailable | can work, quality varies |

Ordinary phone calls and SMS are not affected and keep working even when mobile data is cut. That's your fallback.

## What to set up before you fly

1. **Install and test a VPN at home.** App stores in Russia remove VPN apps, and downloading one on a Russian network is harder. Set it up while you're still abroad and check that Telegram opens through it.
2. **Plan for the first day.** A foreign SIM in Russia has no mobile data and SMS for the first 24 hours, so the first hours may be Wi-Fi only. Read [how the 24-hour block on foreign SIM cards works](/en/blog/foreign-sim-24-hour-block-russia/).
3. **Save the important stuff offline.** Screenshot hotel addresses, booking confirmations, tickets and contact numbers that you'd normally find in Telegram chats. Download offline maps of the cities you'll visit.
4. **Agree on a backup channel.** Ask your hosts, guide or family how to reach you if Telegram is down: a normal phone call, SMS or email. Russian contacts may also use MAX, the state-backed messenger that stays available during restrictions. [WhatsApp is blocked too](/en/blog/does-whatsapp-work-in-russia/), so don't count on it as plan B.
5. **Keep Telegram updated before the trip.** Older versions often handle unstable connections worse.

## If Telegram stops working during the trip

- **Check whether it's the whole internet.** Open our [internet check](/en/check/): if Russian sites open but foreign ones don't, mobile data is probably in whitelist mode. Switch to Wi-Fi.
- **Check the news for your region.** Restrictions are often local and tied to drone alerts. We collect them daily on the [blocking status page](/en/status/), and the [restrictions map](/en/karta/) shows where it's worse right now.
- **Reconnect the VPN, then restart Telegram.** Close the app fully, connect the VPN, open Telegram again. If it still hangs, try another server.
- **Telegram stuck on "Connecting" or "Updating"?** We have a [step-by-step guide](/en/blog/telegram-ne-podklyuchaetsya/).

## Is it legal to use a VPN in Russia?

This is not legal advice. As of late 2026, using a VPN as a private person is not an offence in Russia. Advertising VPN services is banned, and since September 2025 there are fines for knowingly searching for content officially listed as extremist, and using a VPN to do that is treated as an aggravating factor. Ordinary use, like reading your chats or calling home, isn't covered by those rules. Laws change quickly, so check the latest news before your trip.

## FAQ

### Does Telegram work in Moscow and St Petersburg?
Without a VPN, mostly not. Both cities also see frequent mobile internet restrictions, when only whitelisted Russian services work on mobile data.

### Will Telegram work on hotel Wi-Fi?
Usually only with a VPN. Wi-Fi is not affected by mobile shutdowns, so it's the best place to reconnect.

### Can I use Telegram through roaming without a VPN?
Sometimes, but don't rely on it. Results vary by provider, and some say roaming traffic is now filtered too.

### What do people in Russia use instead of Telegram?
Many keep using Telegram through a VPN. Others switched to MAX, VK messenger or plain phone calls and SMS.
