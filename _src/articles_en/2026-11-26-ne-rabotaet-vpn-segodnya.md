First turn the VPN off and try opening a normal website. If nothing loads at all, the problem is the network, not the VPN: check <a href="/en/status/">today's outage status</a>. ## Why your VPN isn't working today

In November 2026 almost every VPN complaint in Russia falls into one of three scenarios.

**1. A mobile internet outage or shutdown.** On 25 November, for example, Moscow and the Moscow region logged over 9,000 internet complaints in a day, and AdGuard VPN was among the services people reported. The same night mobile data was restricted in Samara region during a drone alert, and in Kazan's Azino district phones showed signal but no data went through. At times like these no VPN works: it has no connection to run on.

**2. Whitelist mode.** Mobile data only lets through approved services: Gosuslugi, banks, VK, Yandex, marketplaces. Ordinary VPN servers aren't on that list, so the app hangs on "Connecting…".

**3. The server got blocked.** According to AppleInsider.ru, since August 2026 blocks hit whole hosting subnets rather than single addresses. In early November users started reporting that even personal servers shared by 2-3 people went down, sometimes within hours or days. This hasn't been officially confirmed, but the symptom is the same: it connected yesterday, it doesn't today.

## VPN stopped working: step by step

1. **Turn the VPN off and open any website.** Nothing loads? Then it's not the VPN. Wait, or switch to Wi-Fi.
2. **Refresh the subscription in your app.** Happ, Incy and V2RayTun all have a button to update the server list. The provider may have replaced blocked addresses while you still have the old ones.
3. **Pick another location.** If one server is blocked, the next one often works.
4. **Check the date and time on your phone.** Some protocols won't connect if the clock is off.
5. **Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.** That tells you whether it's your carrier or the VPN.
6. **Restart the app and the phone.** Simple, but after overnight shutdowns the tunnel often gets stuck.
7. **Contact your VPN's support.** Tell them your carrier, city and exactly what you see.

If nothing helps, set the subscription up again using the <a href="/en/podklyuchenie/">setup guide</a>: delete the old one and add it fresh with the link from the bot.

## Why even a VPN fails under whitelists

Under whitelist mode the carrier only lets traffic reach approved addresses. A regular VPN connects to a server abroad, and that server isn't on the list. It's not your app that's broken: almost every service behaves this way. Only providers with a separate option for this mode keep working, and you should test that during a trial rather than trust promises. How to recognise whitelist mode is explained in <a href="/en/blog/belye-spiski-chto-eto/">Internet whitelists in Russia</a>.

## FAQ

### Why is my VPN not working today when it was fine yesterday?

Most often the server you used was blocked, or mobile internet restrictions were switched on in your region. Refresh the subscription and change location. If nothing loads without the VPN either, it's a network outage: see <a href="/en/status/">today's summary</a>.

### My VPN stopped working on my phone but works on my laptop. Why?

Your laptop is probably on home internet while your phone is on mobile data, where restrictions or whitelists apply. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi: if the VPN starts working, it's the carrier.

### Can you be fined for using a VPN in Russia?

As of November 2026 the Code of Administrative Offences has no fine for simply using a VPN. There are fines for advertising VPNs and ways around blocks. Laws change, so keep an eye on the news. This is not legal advice.

### Which VPN works in Russia today?

The one that passes the test where you are: with your carrier and in your city. Take a trial and check it on both mobile data and Wi-Fi. See <a href="/en/blog/kak-vybrat-vpn-2026/">which VPN works in Russia</a> for how to choose.
