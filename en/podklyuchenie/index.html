iPhone with a Russian Apple ID

Apple removed Incy, HAPP and V2RayTun from the Russian App Store. There is no other way to install apps on an iPhone in Russia: installing outside the App Store is only allowed in the EU. What to do depends on your situation:

The app is already installed

Don't delete it - it keeps working. Just update the subscription: @ThuggerVPN_bot → 🌍 Мой VPN (My VPN) → 🔄 Обновить подписку (Update subscription), then paste the link into the app again.

No app yet: an Apple ID from another country

1. Open the App Store → tap your avatar → sign out of "Media & Purchases" only. Don't touch iCloud - your photos and contacts stay.
2. Create a new Apple ID for another country (e.g. Kazakhstan, Turkey, the US) or sign in to an existing one.
3. Download Incy (or HAPP, V2RayTun).
4. Sign out and back into your own Apple ID - the app stays on the phone.
Note: Apple asks for an address in the chosen country, and future app updates will need that same account.

An Apple ID of someone abroad

You can download the app under the account of someone you trust: sign in to "Media & Purchases" only, download the app and sign out. Don't save their password or sign in to their iCloud.

The subscription from the bot works with any of the three apps - install whichever you can download. Stuck on a step? Message support.

If the VPN doesn't work

1. Is your subscription active?

Open @ThuggerVPN_bot → button 🌍 Мой VPN (My VPN) → check the line 📆 Оплачен до (Paid until). If it expired, renew in the bot.

2. Does the subscription have locations?

In @ThuggerVPN_bot → 🌍 Мой VPN check 🌎 Локации в подписке (Locations). If empty, tap 🔄 Обновить подписку (Update subscription).

3. Update the keys

In @ThuggerVPN_bot → 🌍 Мой VPN → 🔄 Обновить подписку and follow the prompts. Then paste the link into the app again.

4. It may be a whitelist

Carriers can temporarily restrict mobile internet so that only approved sites work - government services, banks, major Russian services. If the VPN won't connect or pages won't load, connect to Wi-Fi. You can check what you have in 5 seconds on the check page.
